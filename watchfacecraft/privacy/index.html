Privacy Policy for WatchFaceCraft

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

In short

WatchFaceCraft does not collect any data about you. The app has no server, no user account, no ads and no tracking. Your watch faces stay on your phone and your watch.

What happens on your devices

Building watch faces. The app stores your designs, images and settings only on your phone. The watch face is built on your phone and sent to your watch. None of it reaches us.

Heart rate, steps, battery and complications. The app itself never reads your heart rate, steps or any other health data. If your watch face shows such values, Wear OS reads them on the watch and displays them directly. They never leave the watch, and neither the app nor we can access them. The same applies to complication data you pick on the watch (such as weather or calendar events). That data comes from the apps you choose for it.

Phone-to-watch connection. Phone and watch exchange the watch face through Google's Wear OS data interface (Wearable Data Layer). This uses Bluetooth or, if needed, an end-to-end encrypted connection through Google. Only WatchFaceCraft on your own devices can read this data.

Backup (Android backup)

Android backs up your designs, the app settings (such as when your trial started) and the app's signing key to your Google account. This lets you keep editing and updating your watch faces after you switch phones. The app allows this backup only when it is end-to-end encrypted. No one but you can read it, including us and Google. You can turn backup off in your Android settings.

Buying Pro through Google Play

If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the purchase. Google is responsible for that processing itself; the Google Privacy Policy applies. The app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro and remembers the answer on your phone. From the purchase we only see what Google shows every developer in the Play Console (such as order number, country and amount). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Contacting us by email and error reports

If you email us, we process your email address and message to reply to you. If you paste an error report from the app, it contains the error message (which may include parts of your watch face, such as your own text), the app version, your device model and your Android version. It is never sent automatically, only when you paste and send it yourself. We delete your messages once your request is resolved, unless we are required to keep them.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just send us an email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

Not a medical device

WatchFaceCraft is not a medical device. Values such as heart rate or steps are not intended for diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that makes this policy inaccurate, we will update it. The version on this page always applies.
